Your inbox, sorted on your device.

localsort reads and sorts your mail with an AI model that runs inside this browser. No server sees your messages, your sign-in or the decisions. Arabic and English.

  • On-device. The model (≈460 MB, downloaded once) runs on your GPU or CPU.
  • Verified. Every model file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 before it runs.
  • Careful. If it isn't sure, the email stays in your inbox.
  • Scam checks. Look-alike domains, failed SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the model's own read.
    Mail never leaves this browser.
